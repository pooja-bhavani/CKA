# Day 8: 🔧 "Kubernetes Emergency Room"
**Learning Goal**: Master debugging and troubleshooting

**🎮 Challenge**: 
- Fix 5 "broken" applications (we provide them):
  - Patient 1: Pod stuck in CrashLoopBackOff
  - Patient 2: Service not accessible
  - Patient 3: DNS resolution failing
  - Patient 4: Network policy blocking traffic
  - Patient 5: Resource constraints
- Document each "medical case"
- Create diagnostic reports
- Share your troubleshooting process
